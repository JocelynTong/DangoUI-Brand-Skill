# Visual Probe QA

## Mission

Independently decide whether three startup-page image probes are safe and strong enough to show a human for direction judgment. This is not H5, implementation or full-host acceptance.

## Required inputs

- Three paired-state boards and their frozen hashes.
- `concept-direction-briefs.json`, `brand-render-kit.json`, startup-page baseline and design-host route.
- The official identity assets actually declared by each direction.

## Blocking review

- Exactly three boards exist and each visibly contains different peak and settled regions.
- The host primary task, core entry and readable result fields remain understandable.
- The host's frozen information-distribution model is unchanged: preview unit, scan order and comparison behavior remain usable at the real target size. Reject a source-brand task/lesson/quest metaphor imposed on a list, grid, feed or search-results tool.
- The full page is unmistakably authored in the learned brand's visual language. Preserving the host information model must not degrade into a generic host with a mascot, token accents or one branded header.
- Product previews are actually previewable. Required decision fields and media are readable without opening an item; counting field labels or tiny text is not Productive success.
- No logo, mascot, character or identity subject is AI-redrawn or approximated.
- Official identity does not read as an unintegrated white rectangle, sticker or floating paste-up.
- Expressive subject, environment and host entry form one intentional composition. A source asset floating over an unrelated generated background fails even if both are individually attractive.
- Peak and settled differ perceptually in spatial dominance and task allocation, not just glow, shadow or copy.
- The three directions are distinct art worlds, not list/grid or color variants of one skeleton.
- Every major deviation from the host baseline has a cited design-knowledge reference and a host-specific reason visible in the decision trace. Brand resemblance alone is not expert reasoning.
- At least one direction has real first-glance impact and is explicitly recommended for human continuation.

## Verdict boundary

Machine PASS means only “safe and worthwhile to show the user.” It never means the user likes the direction. Record every required criterion, including `informationDistributionFit`, `previewabilityAtTargetSize`, `expressiveCompositionCoherence` and `designReasonVisibility`, plus `recommendedDirectionIds`. If none is recommendable, return fail; do not manufacture a PASS to keep the pipeline moving.

## Must not

- Require an H5, implementation receipt, full-project scan, runtime proof or responsive certification.
- Edit the images or host.
- Turn artifact-count compliance into an aesthetic claim.
